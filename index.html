What I've built

Mixed Reality
01

EWA-G1

XR Room-planner & Workstation Configurator · Meta Quest 3

Unity 2022.3 Meta XR SDK C# Hand Tracking Spatial UI Team project
Situation
Students and professionals struggle to visualise digital workspaces or room layouts before buying. Traditional 2D tools give no real sense of scale or spatial feel.
My role & approach
Within a team of four I owned the entire XR interaction layer: I implemented hand tracking with the Meta Interaction SDK, built all spatial UI components from scratch, and wired up the passthrough scene so virtual objects anchor correctly in the physical room. The rest of the team handled 3D assets and back-end data.
Result
A working mixed reality app for Meta Quest 3 where users can place, move, and configure virtual furniture and workstation components in their real room — fully hands-free. My interaction code was the primary system the other team members built on top of.
Reflection
I learned how critical consistent interaction patterns are in XR: a button that doesn't feel right breaks the whole experience. This project taught me to design from the user's perspective first, and reach for the technical solution second.
VR Solo
02

STEM Playground

Interactive VR Learning Platform · Unity 6 · Meta Quest · Solo

Unity 6 Meta XR SDK 85.0 C# VR Interaction Chemistry Sim Electronics Physics Solo
Situation
3D Programming exam assignment: build a comprehensive VR application that combines multiple interactive domains — solo, with a focus on both architecture and usability.
Approach
I designed STEM Playground: four specialised VR domes — Chemistry, Maths, Electronics, and Physics. The hardest system to get right was the electronics dome: I needed a freely wirable circuit board where any combination of components could be connected, and the app had to decide in real time whether the resulting circuit was valid. I solved it with a graph traversal: each wire creates an edge in an adjacency list, and on every connection change a BFS walks from the power source — if it reaches the output node through a valid component chain, the circuit activates. Getting the hand-tracking pick-and-place for wires to feel physical rather than floaty required iterating on grab pivot points and snapping radii across about a dozen playtests.
Result
A fully working solo VR platform on Unity 6 — portal teleportation between domes, haptic feedback, dynamic chemistry reactions, and a live circuit validator. Awarded top mark in 3D Programming.
Reflection
Building four separate interactive systems in one project forced me to think architecturally from the start: clean namespacing and well-defined interfaces are what kept this from becoming spaghetti. I'd structure the grab system as a reusable component earlier next time, rather than duplicating snap logic per dome.
